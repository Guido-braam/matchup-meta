The tier follows from the expected winrate: the average of all matchups, weighted by how often you meet each deck (set this in My meta). The cut-offs scale with your meta: the decks you meet are ranked (equal winrates share a place), the top 10% is S, the next 15% A, then 25% B, 25% C, 15% D and the bottom 10% E. Within a tier the highest winrate comes first. Matchups with few games are pulled towards 50%. Click a deck to see its matchups.

The default frequency level (dot under the button) follows the deck's share at Regionals, with recent Regionals counting more and Regionals on your continent 1.5 times. Decks not played there follow their share on Trainerhill. The continent does not change the lists on a deck page. What you change is saved in this browser; Reset puts a deck back to its default. Export settings saves your continent and frequencies to a file you can import in another browser; your API key, Advice, Deck chat and Deck Lab stay out of it.

Claude looks at My meta (the frequency levels you set), all matchups, the Regionals results and the Recommended lists, and advises which deck and which list to play. This uses your own Anthropic API key and credit. An analysis usually takes half a minute to two minutes.
