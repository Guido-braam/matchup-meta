How is this calculated?

The tier follows from the expected winrate: the average of all matchups, weighted by how often you meet each deck (set this in My meta). The cut-offs scale with your meta: the decks you meet are ranked (equal winrates share a place), the top 10% is S, the next 15% A, then 25% B, 25% C, 15% D and the bottom 10% E. Within a tier the highest winrate comes first. Matchups with few games are pulled towards 50%. Click a deck to see its matchups.

Which deck should I play at locals?

Claude looks at My meta (the frequency levels you set), all matchups, the Regionals results and the Recommended lists, and advises which deck and which list to play. This uses your own Anthropic API key and credit. An analysis usually takes half a minute to two minutes.

With a key, Claude helps you in three places: it advises which deck to play at your locals, answers questions about a deck in Deck chat, and turns your Analysis into tips on how to play. Everything else on the site works without a key.

The site calls Claude straight from your browser with your own key, so Anthropic bills your account for what you use. Create a key in the Claude Console and paste it below. The key is stored only in this browser, only sent to Anthropic, and never part of an export.

How is this calculated?

The default frequency level (labelled "default" in a deck's menu) follows the deck's share at Regionals, with recent Regionals counting more and Regionals on your continent 1.5 times. Decks not played there follow their share on Trainerhill. The continent does not change the lists on a deck page. What you change is saved in this browser; Reset puts a deck back to its default. Export saves your continent, frequencies, My lists and games to one file you can import in another browser; your API key, Advice and Deck chat stay out of it.
